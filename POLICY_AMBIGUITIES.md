# Policy Ambiguities Requiring Owner Decision

1. Reward threshold basis
- INR 2,500 threshold inclusive or exclusive of GST?

2. Reward on principal-credit-funded purchases
- Allowed always, conditionally, or disallowed?

3. Bonus redemption cap
- Keep as configurable draft (e.g., 20%) and final launch default?

4. Non-returnable item reward timing
- Release strictly on delivery, or after a short safeguard window?

5. COD policy
- Confirm disabled at launch; if enabled later define fee + verification logic.

6. Top-up availability
- Keep paid top-up fully feature-flagged OFF until legal/tax/gateway approval.

7. Tax rule seed scope
- Confirm initial HSN/tax categories and effective dates for launch catalog.

8. Inventory freshness guardrail
- Max allowed stale snapshot age before checkout block.

9. Referral anti-abuse handling
- Auto-block thresholds vs manual-review-first policy.

10. Milestone behavior
- Repeatable cycles vs once-only per milestone.

11. Return windows by category
- Standard and category exceptions with legal policy text.

12. Legal entity and policy pages
- Final entity name, support contact, and refund/credit legal wording.

13. Inventory feed cadence
- Daily fixed-time sync, near-real-time manual trigger, or both?

14. Geo tracking precision and retention
- Country/state/city granularity and retention period for IP-derived signals.

15. Manual credit governance
- Which roles can approve credit adjustments and what maker-checker threshold applies?

16. Coupon redemption governance
- Which coupon classes require manual approval and SLA for review.

17. Return decision matrix
- Category-specific rules for auto-approve vs manual-approve returns.
